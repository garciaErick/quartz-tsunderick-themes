# Our Game Studio Plan - Steam Publishing

**Goal**: Publish our game on Steam with clear ownership splits, a professional studio name, and low cost.  
We will use: **General Partnership + DBA** (US-based).

### Why This Plan?
- More official than just a casual agreement.
- Clear ownership percentages and IP rights for all 4 of us.
- Professional studio name on Steam (instead of personal names).
- Low cost (no LLC annual fees).
- Steam supports this setup.

### What Each Part Does
- **General Partnership**: Makes the 4 of us legal partners with documented shares.
- **DBA (Doing Business As)**: Lets us use a cool studio name publicly (e.g. "Amigos Games Studio").

### Estimated Costs
- Partnership Agreement: $0 – $150
- DBA Filing: $10 – $100 (usually $20–$60)
- EIN: Free
- **Total upfront**: Usually under $150
- Steam Direct fee: $100 per game (recoupable after $1,000 revenue)

### Step-by-Step Plan

**Phase 1: Preparation (Do this first)**
1. Agree on:
   - Studio name
   - Ownership percentages (e.g. 25% each)
   - Roles and responsibilities
2. Create and sign **Partnership Agreement** (covers ownership, IP assignment, profit splits, etc.)
   - Good templates: 
     - https://eforms.com/partnership-agreement/
     - https://secretaryofstate.com/partnership-agreement/
3. File **DBA** (Fictitious Business Name) in our county/state.
4. Get free **EIN** (for the partnership):  
   https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number
5. Open a bank account that matches our legal name.

**Phase 2: Steam Direct Signup**
1. Go to: https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect
2. Sign in with a Steam account.
3. Sign agreements and pay $100 fee.
4. Complete tax & bank info:
   - Main contact uses W-9 (US citizen)
   - Mexican friends each provide W-8BEN + RFC
5. Wait for Valve approval.

**Phase 3: After Approval**
- Build store page (must be public "Coming Soon" for at least 2 weeks)
- Upload game build
- Wait 30 days after paying the $100 fee
- Valve review (1-5 days)
- Release the game

### Important Notes
- I (US citizen) will be the main Steam contact.
- All money from Steam comes to the partnership / my account, then I distribute according to our percentages.
- We all assign our work (code, art, music, etc.) to the partnership via the agreement.
- This is low-cost and simple. We can upgrade to LLC later if the game makes good money.

Everyone needs to read and agree to the Partnership Agreement before we start.
